Learn AI Writing with Phoebe

Two tracks into writing that still sounds like you

A model gives you 200 fluent words in four seconds, and fluent was never the hard part. Being specific was. Being brief was. Sounding like a person who has decided something was. This course is about the writing that fills a working week and has your name on it - emails, updates, proposals, reports, reviews, the message you have been putting off since Tuesday. A leader track gives managers and reviewers a way to read a document knowing a model touched it, a house voice written down, and a writing standard that raises the bar without adding process. A writer track runs one real week at Cadence, an AI meeting-notes company, where a proposal slips and a decision is needed. A live clarity lab scores real text on seven measures you can check yourself - hedges, passives, rhythm, abstraction, tells, reading grade and specifics - and climbs 48 to 98 while the word count falls from 223 to 102. Then it loses 36 points to the most requested edit in the world.
