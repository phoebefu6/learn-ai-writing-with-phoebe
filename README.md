# learn ai writing with phoebe

Two tracks into writing that still sounds like you, by Phoebe Fu.

**Live:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-ai-writing-with-phoebe/

A model gives you 200 fluent words in four seconds, and fluent was never the hard part. Being specific
was. Being brief was. Sounding like a person who has decided something was. This course is about the
writing that fills a working week and has your name on it.

- **Leader track (6 x 45 min)** - for managers, editors and anyone who approves writing: what changed,
  how to read a document a model touched without playing detective, the house voice written down,
  disclosure and over-reliance, a one-page team standard, and raising the bar by removing process
  rather than adding it.
- **Writer track (10 x 45 min)** - for anyone whose name goes on the writing: one real week at Cadence
  where a proposal slips and a decision is needed, through briefs, voice capture, emails, memos,
  editing, difficult messages and long-form, ending with a week of your own writing scored.

## The clarity lab

`assets/write-live.js` measures real text on seven computed checks: hedge and filler density, passive
voice, sentence-length variance (the flat-rhythm tell), nominalisation density, AI-tell phrases, a real
**Flesch-Kincaid grade** with syllable counting, and the share of sentences carrying a number or a
name. Turn on the five practices and the score climbs **48 → 52 → 68 → 70 → 83 → 98** while the word
count falls from **223 to 102**. Then press the sixth, "sound more professional", and a finished draft
loses **36 points** and gains 42 words, because every substitution it makes removes a person from a
sentence.

There is a third mode - **score your own writing**. Paste an email you actually sent and the identical
seven measures run on it, entirely in your browser. That box is the part of the course you keep.

The drafts are written examples: one good version plus one specific kind of damage per missing
practice. Every number is computed from the text in front of you. The passive-voice check is a
heuristic, and the pages say so.

## Scope

Deliberately not duplicated: **AI + Content** owns marketing and publishing, **Tech Writing** owns
documentation, **Prompt Engineering** owns prompting as a discipline. This course owns the writing you
are personally accountable for, and the fact that your voice has to survive the tool.

Where the course cites evidence it cites primary sources - the published Flesch-Kincaid formula, the
research on generated-output homogenisation and felt ownership, verification rates from usability
research, and the publishing bodies' actual AI-disclosure requirements (which agree AI cannot be an
author and disagree about where you disclose). See `materials/official-course-map.md`, which also
records what was deliberately left uncited rather than filled with an invented statistic.
